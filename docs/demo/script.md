# WORLDLINE demo script

Spoken, first person, about 400 words. Tabs open in order. Every number appears on screen as it is said.

---

**[Tab, Story landing]**

I want to show you a class of bug that has never had a test. Your pipeline is green. Your agent wrote a nice explanation. And the system still makes the wrong call, because the message that would have changed everything showed up ninety seconds late. Nobody tests for that. We built the thing that does.

**[Tab, Story step 1]**

One hour of a harbour alert, replayed 133 times, each time with the world slightly different. Forty three of those worlds break a safety rule. Our guided search hits a wrong decision by the sixth world. Random search never finds it.

**[Tab, Story step 2]**

Look at this one. Top lane is what really happened. Middle lane is what the duty officer actually knew. Watch the correction slide past the moment he decides. He escalates on a radar contact that had already been retracted. His logic was perfect. His information was late. That is the bug, and it is everywhere.

**[Tab, Story step 3]**

We do not hand you a pile of failing runs. Three changes produced that world, delta debugging keeps the one that matters and proves nothing smaller still breaks it. It even solves the exact threshold. Ninety point two seconds.

**[Tab, Story step 4]**

Then we strip the names out and keep the mechanism. The last correction a decider receives, held past the decision. No port, no officer, no ids. A failure you can carry.

**[Tab, Story step 5]**

So we carried it. A logistics convoy it had never seen. Reproduced. A hospital triage board. Reproduced. The real USGS record of the Kamchatka magnitude eight point eight, real timestamps, real revisions. Reproduced. A live HTTP service, driven request by request. Reproduced. The twin we built to be immune. Not reproduced. Exactly as it should be.

**[Tab, GitLab merge request 1]**

Inside GitLab the agents do the work and nobody lets them grade it. The Investigator reproduced the failure. The Repairer opened this merge request. Code Review caught that the fix was hollow. The Repairer fixed that. Our Skeptic still rejected it, and it was right. This merge request is open on purpose.

**[Tab, Story step 6]**

Every claim is typed. Validated, attested, failed, unknown. Thirty one signed records, each recomputed by the kernel before it attests anything. Now I change one record. Denied. And it tells you which one.

**[Tab, Story step 7]**

You do not have to believe me. Drop the bundle in. Intact. Check the release against its commit. Authorized. Public key only.

**[Tab, Story landing]**

Late information is the oldest failure in operations. WORLDLINE hunts it, proves it, carries it to the next system, and refuses to ship until the evidence clears. This is WORLDLINE.

---

Delivery. Slow down on "His information was late." Land "Reproduced" four times with the same weight. Stop talking the moment "Denied" appears. Finish standing still on the landing page.
