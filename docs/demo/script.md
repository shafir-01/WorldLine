# WORLDLINE demo script

**[Tab, Story landing]**

I want to show you a class of bug that has never had a test, because your pipeline is green, your agent wrote a convincing explanation, and the system still makes the wrong call when the one message that would have changed everything shows up ninety seconds late. Nobody tests for that, so we built the thing that does.

**[Tab, Story step 1]**

Here is one hour of a harbour alert replayed 133 times with the world slightly different each time, and forty three of those worlds break a safety rule that the original run never touched. Our guided search hits a wrong decision by the sixth world, while random search burns its whole budget and never finds it.

**[Tab, Story step 2]**

Look at this one, where the top lane is what really happened and the middle lane is what the duty officer actually knew, and watch the correction slide past the moment he decides. He escalates on a radar contact that had already been retracted, so his logic was perfect while his information was late, and that is the bug we are hunting because it lives in every operational system you run.

**[Tab, Story step 3]**

We do not hand you a pile of failing runs, because three changes produced that world and delta debugging keeps the one that matters while proving that nothing smaller still breaks it, and then it solves the exact threshold at ninety point two seconds so you know precisely where safe ends.

**[Tab, Story step 4]**

Then we strip the names out and keep the mechanism, the last correction a decider receives held past the decision, with no port and no officer and no ids in it, so the failure becomes something you can carry to a system that has never seen it.

**[Tab, Story step 5]**

So we carried it, and a logistics convoy reproduced it, a hospital triage board reproduced it, the real USGS record of the Kamchatka magnitude eight point eight with its real timestamps and real revisions reproduced it, and a live HTTP service driven request by request reproduced it, while the twin we built to be immune did not, exactly as it should.

**[Tab, GitLab merge request 1]**

Inside GitLab the agents do the work and nobody lets them grade it, so the Investigator reproduced the failure, the Repairer opened this merge request, Code Review caught that the fix was hollow, the Repairer fixed that, and our Skeptic still rejected it because it was right to. This merge request is open on purpose, since honest automation shows you where it stopped.

**[Tab, Story step 6]**

Every claim is typed as validated, attested, failed or unknown, and these thirty one signed records were each recomputed by the kernel before it attested anything, so when I change a single record the release is denied and it tells you which one broke.

**[Tab, Story step 7]**

You do not have to believe me, because you can drop the bundle in and see it come back intact, then check the release against its commit and watch it come back authorized, with nothing but a public key between you and the answer.

**[Tab, Story landing]**

Late information is the oldest failure in operations, and WORLDLINE is the first pipeline stage that hunts it, proves it, carries it to the next system, and refuses to ship until the evidence clears. This is WORLDLINE.
